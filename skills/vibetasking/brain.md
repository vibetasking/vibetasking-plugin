# The company brain

Some workspaces belong to a company with a **brain**: shared organizational knowledge accumulated as facts about the company's people, teams, customers, tools, products and processes, each carrying a trust tier (VERIFIED: a member vouched, GROUNDED: the company's own material or a member's own words, UNVERIFIED: the web, an outsider or an agent's own words), a validity window and where it came from. `recall_facts` searches it with a natural-language query and returns ranked facts with their trust, validity, origin and a supporting quote, plus an entity description when the hits concentrate. Facts marked superseded are no longer current truth. Pass ``since`` (an ISO moment) to list what the brain recorded or retracted since then, retracted facts included, with an empty query for every change. Consult it before asking the user something the company likely already answered. If the call answers 404 the brain is not in this run, because the workspace's company has none or the workspace keeps it out of its runs, so skip the brain for the rest of the session.

Around it: `get_entity` reads one entity by name or alias (its description and current facts). `remember_fact` records what the user stated as a company fact, ``stated_by`` member, with the entities it concerns and a short verbatim quote, and the reply lists the facts the brain already holds that look like it, and you settle them on the spot: `retract_fact` the old one naming the new as its replacement when the user corrected it, or the new one when it only repeats an old one, and a neighbour marked retracted was retracted by a member and stands, so retract the new one when it restates it. `verify_fact` records that the user vouched for a fact in so many words. `request_fact_confirmation` puts a one-click card in front of the user (verify, make public, make private) after they agreed in the conversation, never to nudge, one card per fact at a time, and their click records it with their name on your next turn. `ask_company_question` files a question for the members, `raise_brain_finding` lands a recommendation, risk, opportunity or automation candidate through the evidence gate (two current facts from two origins) on the user's instruction, and `handoff_brain_finding` turns an open automation or recommendation finding into a build conversation. `merge_brain_entities` folds two entities that are one thing, and `unmerge_brain_entities` brings a merged one back when that was wrong. `update_entity` edits an entity's name, aliases or description, `forget_brain_facts` (a workspace ADMIN) forgets everything the brain learned from one origin, only reporting what would go until the dry run is turned off, and `list_brain_passes` lists the research passes with what each read and wrote. Facts, entities and findings are also readable rows in the data plane, and the ``edits`` rows hold their history.

The settings a member may change, the research agenda and the switches that put the brain in a workspace's runs are on the company record (see **Company record and brain** in `operations.md`).
